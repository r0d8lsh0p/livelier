here's exactly what's going on ↓
what just happened

Your stream has fans beyond your server.

You listed your server in the public Owncast directory — Livelier watches it. When you go live, we announce your stream to Nostr, an open social network with apps full of people who love independent live video.

And when one of those people opens your stream's chat, Livelier joins your chat too — under its own name, never in disguise. That join is our introduction. If you saw "Livelier" appear, it means someone out there just tuned in.

more reach

Go live once. Get discovered everywhere.

The moment you're live, your stream appears in every app that speaks the open live-streaming standard — with your title, your artwork, and a player pointed straight at your server. Nothing is re-hosted, nothing is uploaded. New viewers, same self-hosted stream.

Zap.Stream Amethyst Primal Shosho …any NIP-53 app

No account to create. No algorithm to appease. Just an open protocol doing what open protocols do.

chat, both ways

One conversation, two worlds.

For channels that switch it on, chat flows in both directions: messages from Nostr viewers appear in your Owncast chat with their real names, and your community's replies reach them right back. Nobody has to leave home to hang out together.

the part that matters

Built around your community's trust.

We built an earlier version of this bridge, showed it to streamers, and listened hard to what they told us. The result is a short list of promises that are enforced by the architecture — not by good intentions.

Your viewers' words don't persist.

Mirrored chat lives on one dedicated relay, is marked do-not-share at the protocol level, and self-destructs after three hours. It is never archived, never syndicated, never searchable later.

Streamers told us: "I can't promise my chat won't live forever somewhere." Now you can.

No company in your chat.

Livelier is a community project with a name of its own. It joins your chat as itself, attaches no brand to you or your viewers, and mirrors nothing under a corporate flag.

Streamers told us: "a company injected itself into my chat." Never again.

No impersonation, ever.

Everything we publish is clearly labelled as a bridge and links back to your server as the source. Your channel keeps one stable identity that is honestly marked as a mirror — not a fake "you".

Your stream, your rules.

We never edit, filter, or gate your content. Marked your stream adult? We pass the label along and let viewers' apps handle it. And we're feather-light on your server: one playlist check per minute — about a twentieth of a single viewer — and we only connect to chat when someone is actually watching.

you're in control

Lean in, or wave us off. One message either way.

Do nothing Your live streams stay discoverable to Nostr viewers, exactly as they already are through the public directory you listed yourself in.

Turn chat on Two-way chat runs on a per-channel allowlist — it only ever switches on for your stream deliberately. Ask, and we'll flip it.

Opt out entirely Want your instance left alone? Say the word and we remove it — and its history — from the bridge.

free & open source

Community-driven. Auditable. Forkable.

Every line of Livelier is open source — the bridge, the relays, the promises above. Audit exactly what we publish about your stream. Run your own bridge if you'd rather. Or join in: the architecture is built for adding new networks, and PeerTube is next on the wishlist.
