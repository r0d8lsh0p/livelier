# Livelier brand guide

## What livelier is

Livelier is a free open source community project to create livelier livestreams on the open internet.

You're live! Live! Live! But where is your live chat? There's no one here – yet. As a livestreamer you need to be discovered the moment you go live. That makes things hard for streamers who would self-host and own their own live streaming setup, because they have not had the same reach as the larger streaming platforms. Livelier is a project that intends to bring that reach.

Livelier is a bridge, from self-host streaming solutions (like Owncast) to the Nostr network where live streams get syndicated across a global network of sites and apps to get discovered fast.

Live stream videos are bridged to the network, but all viewers hit your server. Live chats are bridged both ways so that audience gets the full live chat experience whether they are watching on your server's hosted chat, or anywhere else on the network.

Livelier has a free to use public instance at livelier.live or you can fork and self-host your own bridge to publish your lives and chats to the Nostr network.

Great Vibes (available on Google Fonts)

## The Livelier logo mark

The words, "Livelier.live"

The letter "L" features arrows pointing both ways, representing the two-way chat bridge. The swirls give movement, and they cross the boundary of the vertical shaft of the L, representing a bridge from one side to the other.

## Other design elements to use

- Movement with elements that animate in
- Hand-drawn looking arrows that curl around before pointing to their target
